SOILL Assistant — privacy notice

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Who we are

This chat assistant is provided as part of the SOILL project (EU Mission Soil). For privacy questions or to request deletion of your chat history, contact the SOILL project team via the project website or your usual Cranfield University / SOILL project contact.

What we collect

When you use the assistant we store:

  • the text of your questions and the assistant’s replies
  • a randomly generated session ID (kept in your browser for this tab only, and cleared when you start a “New chat”)
  • a technical visitor fingerprint derived from connection details (used only to understand usage patterns and help prevent abuse)
  • the date and time of each message

We do not create user accounts. We do not store your name, email address, or other contact details unless you type them into the chat yourself. On the production service we do not store your raw IP address or browser user-agent string in the conversation database.

Why we process this data

We process chat content to answer your questions, keep short conversation context, and monitor and improve the service. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating and improving a research and project support tool. Please do not enter personal data about yourself or others into the chat.

Who processes your messages

Your questions (and recent conversation turns needed for context) are sent to Mistral AI to generate embeddings and answers. Mistral acts as a processor for this purpose. The application and database are hosted on Render.

How long we keep data

Chat records are retained for 12 months, after which they are deleted automatically. Session IDs in your browser expire when you close the tab or start a new chat.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including UK GDPR / EU GDPR), you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the UK: the Information Commissioner’s Office). To exercise these rights in relation to chat logs, contact us using the details above and include approximate dates of use if possible.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
