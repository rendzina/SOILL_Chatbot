SOILL Assistant — privacy notice

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Who we are

This chat assistant is provided as part of the SOILL project (EU Mission Soil). For privacy questions, contact soill@cranfield.ac.uk.

What we collect

When you use the assistant we store:

  • the text of your questions and the assistant’s replies
  • a randomly generated session ID (kept in your browser for this tab only, and cleared when you start a “New chat”)
  • the date and time of each message

We do not create user accounts. We do not store IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, names, email addresses, or other contact details. Please do not type personal data about yourself or others into the chat.

Why we process this data

We store chat content to answer your questions, keep short conversation context within a session, and monitor and improve the service as part of the SOILL project.

Who processes your messages

Your questions (and recent conversation turns needed for context) are sent to Mistral AI to generate embeddings and answers. The application and database are hosted on Render.

How long we keep data

Chat records are retained for 12 months, after which they are deleted automatically. Session IDs in your browser expire when you close the tab or start a new chat.

Further information

If you have questions about this notice, email soill@cranfield.ac.uk. You may also contact Cranfield University’s information assurance team or lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) where applicable.

Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.
